# *How to play Unora Card Battle TCG*
Greetings everyone, and welcome to the Unora TCG beginner's guide, written by Iglis Viriou.
In this guide I will be going over all of the necessary rules to begin playing the game.

## Getting Started
To begin, you will need a deck.
By completing the tutorial, you are given a starter deck to work with.
If you wish to craft your own in the future, the requirements of any deck is that you have exactly 40 cards, no more and no less, with no more than 3 copies of any given card.

## Card Types and How to read cards
Cards are broken into 4 different types: Creature, Spell, Instant, and Trap. I will go over the differences in a moment, but first we need to know how to read a card.

[Cultist](./tcg/neutral/cultist.png)

Each card has a name in the top left, a mana cost in the top right, a card type in the middle underneath the art, and a text box.
Creature cards also have a power value in the bottom left and a toughness value in the bottom right.

[image of a Cultist card with all of this information pointed out]

A colored square indicates a mana of a chosen element. Red being Fire, blue being Water, green being Wind, and brown being Earth.
Each colored mana will be its own individual box. Any mana cost that is indicated by a number means you need that much mana of ANY color.
The cultist above has a mana cost of 1 mana of any color.
The card below, Blue Blob, has a mana cost of 1 Water (blue) mana and 2 additional mana of any element/color.

[image of a Blue Blob card]

Creatures, spells, and traps can be played only on your turn during the first or second Main Phases. This is also commonly known as "Sorcery Speed." Traps, like the name implies however, are set and activate whenever their activation requirement is met. Creatures also cannot attack the turn they are cast, unless they have Haste in their effect text. Instants can be played at any time, during either player's turn, in response to most things. ## Basic rules during play
During each turn, you are allowed to add 1 mana crystal to your mana pool. To do so, select a card in your hand to convert into a mana crystal. Note that the card will no longer be accessible for the remainder of the battle. It will be a mana crystal for the rest of the game. However, you are able to spend as much mana as you like, so long as you have the appropriate mana crystals to pay for your spells.
Outside of playing cards as Mana, you are allowed to play as many cards as you like per turn, assuming you have enough mana to cast them all.
Each player starts with 7 cards and 20 life. The going 2nd player also gains an additional life to make up for the advantage of setting up uninterrupted that the going first player has.
Before the first turn, each player is allowed to put back any number of cards from their hand into their deck and redraw that many cards. This is known as a Mulligan, and you are allowed a single mulligan per game. If you choose to keep every card, you are not allowed to mulligan later in the game. You either use it or lose it.

## Phases of play
The phases of the game go as follows:

Untap step - This is the step where all of your creatures that attacked last turn, or are otherwise "tapped" (unable to attack, block, or activate their abilities), are made available again. In addition, all of your empty/used mana crystals replenish themselves.
Upkeep step - Currently, there are no cards that deal with the Upkeep step, and it may not even be currently present in the game. However, as the Unora Card Battle TCG is based off of Magic: The Gathering primarily, I assume it is and am putting this here for clarity.
Main Phase 1 - This is the first Main Phase of your turn, where you are allowed to add a mana crystal and play spells.
Combat Phase - This is broken up into a few different steps: Declaring attackers, declaring blockers, and damage. At each step in this phase, players can activate instants as the wish.
Main Phase 2 - This phase is the exact same as Main Phase 1. Note that you cannot add a mana crystal in this phase if you did so in Main Phase 1, only if you chose not to do so.
End Phase - This is the phase where you end your turn and discard down to the hand size limit.

## Conclusion
With this, you should know all of the basics to begin playing the Unora Card Battle TCG!
I hope you enjoy playing cards with other Aislings, and that this guide was helpful to those who need it!
To win, you must either reduce the opponent's life total to 0, or have the opponent attempt to draw from their deck while no cards remain there.
