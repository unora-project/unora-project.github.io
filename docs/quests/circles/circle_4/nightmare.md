# Nightmare

!!! note "Unfinished for most classes!"

     This quest changes based on what class you're playing, so this guide may not be helpful if you're playing a different class.

## Female Wizard

- Head to the Piet Priestess (30,55)
- Speak to Narve and select "Morrigu Legend" to start the quest
- Select "Where should I go?"

!!! info ""

Narve explains that Morrigu was the ancient triple goddess. She was a Tuatha De Danaan and rumored to be the dark queen of the Faeries. She mentions at the very center of the Enchanted Garden grows a large tree and a patch of bloody flowers. Sing the Song of Morrigu, but be careful; she will claim you.

!!! info ""

- Head to the East Woodlands
- Enter the Enchanted Gardens
- Head to 50,53 for a pop-up
- Select "\*sings Song of Morrigu\*"
- Select "Black crow, over the flower"
- Select "follow the river flow."
- Select "The washer at the ford sours"
- Select "the clothes with blood's flow."
- Select "Battle-bound warriors pass by"
- Select "As tattered leaves fall."
- Select "You wash the garb with lye"
- Select "of those about to fall."
- Select "\*collapses\*"
- Select "\*dreams of Morrigu\*"

You are teleported into a personal nightmare where you must defeat 25 Nightmares to end it.

## Male Wizard

- Head to the Temple of Choosing in Mileth Village Way (9,4)
- Speak to Gregory and select "What do you know about these Nightmares?" to start the quest
- Select "Where should I go?"
- Select "Who is buried there?"
- Select "What should I do?"

!!! info ""

Gregory explains that a Cthonic Disciple was buried in Mileth Crypt long ago, then whispers and calls them "The forbidden one," and "He Who need Not Be Named." He tells you to carry one each of the essences of Theselene, Miraelis, Serendael, and Skandara while you approach the coffin, in the deepest floor of the crypt, that the Cthonic Disciple is buried in, saying their guidance will allow you to hear him.

!!! info ""

- Head to the Mileth Crypt
- Make your way to the center of Floor 15
- Head to 24,21 for a pop-up
- Select "Listen Carefully"
- Select "Enter my Nightmares"

You are teleported into a personal nightmare where you must defeat 25 Nightmares to end it.
